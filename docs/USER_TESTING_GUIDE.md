# User Testing Guide

Thank you for participating in the PharmaPro beta test! This guide provides a structured way for you to explore the tool and provide feedback.

## Testing Objectives
1. Verify ease of navigation.
2. Confirm accuracy of stock deduction and billing.
3. Evaluate the clarity of reports.

---

## Test Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Initial Setup & Inventory Onboarding
**Goal**: Set up the environment and add your first products.
1. Log in to the system.
2. Go to **Settings** and update your shop details.
3. Add 3 different medicines to the inventory.
4. Add 2 different batches for one of the medicines (one expiring in 6 months, one in 2 years).

### Scenario 2: The "Rush Hour" Billing Test
**Goal**: Test the speed and accuracy of the POS.
1. Go to **Billing POS**.
2. Create 3 bills for different "patients".
3. For one bill, apply a 10% discount.
4. For another bill, use the "Credit" payment mode.
5. Verify that the total amount calculated matches your manual calculation.

### Scenario 3: Inventory Maintenance
**Goal**: Test logic and edge cases.
1. Check the **Dashboard** to see if your newly added "Low Stock" items appear.
2. Go to **Bill History** and perform a return on one of the items from Scenario 2.
3. Verify that the inventory count for that item has increased correctly.
4. Go to the **Reports** section and download/view the GSTR-1 summary.

---

## Feedback Template

Please provide your feedback using the following format:

- **What worked well?**
- **What was confusing or difficult?**
- **Did you encounter any bugs?** (Please describe the steps to reproduce)
- **What feature would you like to see next?**

---

## Reporting Bugs
If the application crashes or behaves unexpectedly, please take a screenshot and note down the exact action you were performing.
