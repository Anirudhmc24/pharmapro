# PharmaPro – Smart Pharmacy Management

**Your all‑in‑one solution for inventory, billing, and compliance**

---

## Why PharmaPro?
- **Rapid invoice scanning** – upload multiple invoice images; batch numbers, expiry dates, and medicine details are extracted automatically.
- **One‑click WhatsApp sharing** – instantly send the bill to the customer and store their contact details.
- **Smart stock management** – FEFO batch handling, low‑stock & expiry alerts keep you compliant.
- **Master‑database auto‑fill** – >250 k medicines with composition, manufacturer, and MRP.
- **Dynamic shop layout mapping** – map shelves, boxes, and fixtures for quick retrieval.
- **Regulatory compliance** – GSTR‑1 reports, drug‑interaction warnings, batch traceability.
- **Portable executable** – single‑file Windows `.exe` – no Python install required.
