# 🏆 PharmaPro Master Walkthrough

Welcome to the **Intense Master Guide** for PharmaPro. This guide covers every module, every button, and the "why" behind every feature to ensure you can maximize the efficiency of your pharmacy.

---

## 📦 1. Inventory & FEFO Management
Managing stock is the core of any pharmacy. PharmaPro uses **FEFO (First-Expiry-First-Out)** to ensure you always sell the stock that expires soonest.

### **Key Features & Why to Use Them:**
*   **+ Add Drug**: Create a new medicine entry. 
    *   *Why?* It sets up the HSN codes and categories once so that billing is faster later.
*   **Add Batch**: Enter new stock for an existing drug.
    *   *Why?* This is where you enter **Expiry Dates**. The system uses this to prioritize sales and warn you when stock is getting old.
*   **📍 Locate**: Shows you exactly where a drug is on your 2D Store Map.
    *   *Why?* Helps new staff find medicines instantly without searching through every rack.
*   **Return Expiring Stock**: Generates a return note for stock expiring within 3-6 months.
    *   *Why?* Allows you to return items to suppliers before they become dead stock, saving you money.

## 🧾 2. The POS (Point of Sale) Billing
The billing screen is designed for speed and safety. It checks for **Drug Interactions** in real-time as you add items.

### **Key Features & Why to Use Them:**
*   **Smart Search**: Search by Name, Brand, or Composition.
    *   *Why?* If a specific brand is out of stock, you can instantly find an alternative with the same composition to suggest to the patient.
*   **BOGO / Offers**: Automatically calculates 'Buy One Get One' or other schemes.
    *   *Why?* Ensures you never lose money on schemes and the customer gets the correct deal.
*   **Interaction Alerts**: Shows a warning if two drugs in the cart shouldn't be taken together.
    *   *Why?* Provides a layer of safety for the pharmacist and the patient.
*   **Prescription Upload**: Attach a photo of the doctor's prescription directly to the bill.
    *   *Why?* Critical for compliance with Schedule H/H1 drugs.
*   **Print Bill / Print Challan**: Generate a GST-ready invoice or a Delivery Challan.
    *   *Why?* Professional billing for customers and dispatch notes for home delivery.

## 📊 3. Compliance & Reporting
PharmaPro handles the "boring" accounting stuff for you automatically.

### **Key Features & Why to Use Them:**
*   **GSTR-1 HSN Summary**: View your sales summarized by HSN codes.
    *   *Why?* Makes your monthly GST filing a 5-minute task instead of a 5-hour one.
*   **Schedule H/X Log**: A specialized log for restricted medicines.
    *   *Why?* Required by law. If a drug inspector visits, you have your records ready in one click.
*   **Expiry Alerts**: A dashboard view of what's expiring this month.
    *   *Why?* Lets you run clearance sales or process returns proactively.

## ⚙️ 4. Settings & Automation
This is where you make the app your own.

### **Key Features & Why to Use Them:**
*   **Store Layout Builder**: A drag-and-drop tool to map your racks and boxes.
    *   *Why?* This powers the 'Locate' feature. Map your shop once, and the app does the rest.
*   **Cloud Backup**: Connected to your personal Google Drive.
    *   *Why?* **"Every change matters."** Whenever you bill or add stock, the data is synced. If your computer crashes, your business doesn't stop.
*   **Staff Roles**: Create accounts for pharmacists and cashiers.
    *   *Why?* Admin can see everything, but staff can only bill. Protects your sensitive financial data.

## 💡 Pro Tips for Efficiency:
1.  **Use Shortcuts**: Use `Tab` and `Enter` in the Billing screen to finish a bill without touching the mouse.
2.  **Monitor Backorders**: If a customer asks for a drug that is out of stock, use the **"Notify Me"** button. The app will remind you to call them when the stock arrives.
3.  **Manual Backup**: Before any major software update or shop audit, click **"Trigger Manual Backup"** in Settings just to be extra safe.
